Parakh Sharma

Hello! I'm a student at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, located in the beautiful Powai campus. This website chronicles my incredible journey at one of India's premier engineering institutions, from my first day on campus to the present moment of continued growth and learning.

IITB has been more than just an academic institution - it's been my home, my community, and the place where I've discovered my passion for technology, made lifelong friendships, and grown both personally and professionally.

My Journey Timeline

Key milestones and memorable moments at IITB

July 2024

First Day at IITB

Arrived at the beautiful Powai campus, amazed by the lush greenery and Powai lake views. First glimpse of the famous Infinite Corridor.

August 2024

Hostel Life Begins

Settled into hostel accommodation, made first friends, experienced the famous mess food and late-night study sessions.

October 2024

Academic Foundation

Completed first semester courses, discovered passion for Computer Science and Mathematics. Adjusted to the rigorous academic environment.

December 2024

Inter IIT Sports Meet 2024

Participated in the annual sports tournament of the Indian Institutes of Technology. It is organized in December, with the Aquatics events held separately in October. It is the longest-running Inter-collegiate meet where all IITs participate, having been held since 1961. This year (2024), the event was held at IIT Kanpur and IIT Indore jointly.

December 2024

TechFest Experience

Engaged in technical competitions, attended workshops, and showcased projects. Gained exposure to cutting-edge technology and innovation.

December 2024

Mood Indigo 2024

Participated in Asia's largest cultural festival. Witnessed amazing performances, made memories with friends, and experienced the vibrant IITB culture.

March 2025

Research Project

Started working on first research project under faculty guidance. Learned about academic research methodologies and scientific writing.

June 2025

Summer Internship

Completed internship at a tech company, applied classroom learning to real-world problems, gained industry experience.

Present

Continued Growth

Currently pursuing advanced courses, involved in multiple clubs, and preparing for future opportunities in technology and research.
